# AgentLens — Demo Video Showcase Guide

A simple, shot-by-shot script for recording the submission video.
Target length: **2.5–3 minutes**. You don't have to say the lines word-for-word —
they're there so you never have to think about what to say next.

Live site: **https://agentlens-ashy.vercel.app/**
(Backup URL if that's slow: https://agentlens-temmygabriels-projects.vercel.app/ — same site.)

> the **four categories** the judges require (Rebalancing · Grid Trading · Yield
> Optimisation · Health Factor Monitoring), the **Hire** button is now real and
> on screen, and the numbers are bigger (**434 agents, ~114 live**). This script

## The one-sentence pitch (memorize this)

> "AgentLens is the marketplace for BNB's agent economy where you can **find the
> right agent by the job you need, see proof it actually works, and hire it in a
> few clicks** — with nothing faked."

That single idea is what wins. Everything in the video should support it.

---

## Before you hit record (2-minute checklist)

- [ ] Open the site and **do a fresh page load** so the numbers are current.
- [ ] Pick your theme: **dark mode looks the most impressive on video** (click the 🌙 toggle, top-right). Be consistent.
- [ ] Zoom the browser to **110–125%** (Ctrl and +) so text is big and readable on video.
- [ ] Hide your bookmarks bar and any messy browser tabs.
- [ ] **Scout one good live agent first.** This is the most important prep step:
  1. From the home page, click the **Yield Optimisation** tile (it has the most live agents — a full grid of green looks great on camera). *Rebalancing works well too if you prefer that story.*
  2. On the category page, click the **"Live now"** filter.
  3. Open the first agent, confirm its health gauge is high and it has a **green, reachable service**, then keep that tab. That's the agent you'll hire on camera — so you never land on a dead one.
- [ ] Turn on **Do Not Disturb** so notifications don't pop up mid-record.

## The winning flow (what the judges asked for)

The judges want to watch one person **land → find an agent by category →
understand it → hire it — with no dead ends.** The scenes below are exactly that
journey, in order. If you do nothing else, make Scenes 2 → 3 → 4 feel smooth and
fast. That flow *is* your strongest argument.

---

### 🎬 Scene 1 — The hook (0:00–0:25) · Home page

**SHOW:** The home page. Let the hero sit a second so the **pulsing green dot** and the reachability line are visible, then let the **four category tiles** show on screen.

**SAY:**
> "There are hundreds of thousands of AI agents registered on BNB Smart Chain — but which ones are real, and which one do you actually need? This is AgentLens. It indexes **434 agents**, and right now it's live-checked every one — only about **114 are reachable this second**. It sorts them into the four jobs that matter: rebalancing, grid trading, yield, and health-factor monitoring."

*(Say whatever number your fresh page load shows — specifics feel true.)*

**Why this scene wins:** it opens with the problem (noise), shows **real, honest data** in the first 20 seconds (Data Quality), and shows the **four categories** front and center (Agent Diversity) — before you've even clicked.

---

### 🎬 Scene 2 — Find an agent by category (0:25–0:55) · Category page

**SHOW:** Click the **Yield Optimisation** tile (or Rebalancing). Let the category page load — the hero explains the job, and a grid of real agents appears. Click the **"Live now"** filter.

**SAY:**
> "I don't need to know a single contract address. I pick the job — say, yield optimisation — and AgentLens shows me every agent that does it. One click hides everything that isn't reachable right now, so I'm only looking at agents I can actually use. No dead ends."

**Why this scene wins:** this is the **"find an agent in a few clicks"** measure and the **category** requirement in one shot — the single most important thing the judges said they're looking for.

---

### 🎬 Scene 3 — Understand the agent (0:55–1:40) · Agent detail page

**SHOW:** Click the live agent you scouted. Let the detail page load — the **health gauge** animates up to its score.

**SAY:**
> "Before I trust any agent, I open its evidence page. AgentLens gives it a live health score — and this was **checked seconds ago**, not cached."

**SHOW:** Point at the **"What it claims" vs "✓ What AgentLens verified"** split panel.

**SAY:**
> "This is the heart of it. On the left, what the agent *claims* about itself. On the right, what AgentLens **actually verified** by pinging its real endpoints. We never invent a trust score — we show you the difference between talk and proof."

**SHOW:** Scroll to the **Verification evidence** list (green ✓ / red ✕ per service). Click **"Verify again"** to prove it's live on demand.

**SAY:**
> "Every service it declares gets a live reachability check — and I can re-run that check myself, right now."

**Why this scene wins:** **Functionality + Data Quality** together — a working, live verification pipeline, not a mockup.

---

### 🎬 Scene 4 — Hire it in a few clicks (1:40–2:15) · Hire flow ⭐ NEW

**SHOW:** Click the big **⚡ Hire this agent** button. The hire panel opens. Point at the **verified live endpoint**, click **Copy**, and hover the **Open ↗** link. If an **x402** badge shows, point at it.

**SAY:**
> "And here's the part most directories skip — actually hiring it. Because AgentLens already verified this agent, it hands me its live connection directly. One click to copy the endpoint, one to open it. If the agent takes pay-per-call payments, it says so. No guesswork, no dead ends."

**SHOW:** Point at the honest note at the bottom of the panel about on-chain hiring.

**SAY (optional):**
> "And this is where on-chain hiring plugs in next — the agent transacting for itself inside spending limits you set."

**Why this scene wins:** "activate in a few clicks" is now a **core** judging point. This scene closes the whole journey — and it's honest: you're handing over a connection you *proved* works, not faking a transaction.

---

### 🎬 Scene 5 — Diversity across all four categories (2:15–2:40)

**SHOW:** Go back to the home page. Slowly pass over all **four category tiles** so each one's live count is visible. Optionally open a second category briefly, or add two agents to **Compare** and open the compare view.

**SAY:**
> "AgentLens covers all four jobs as first-class categories — and it shows the *honest* supply in each. Yield is crowded; grid bots and health-monitors are rarer. We don't pad the shelves — showing you the real market **is** the data quality."

**Why this scene wins:** this is **Agent Diversity** — breadth across all four required categories — plus a confident, honest answer to "why do some categories have fewer agents?"

---

### 🎬 Scene 6 — Close (2:40–3:00)

**SHOW:** Back to the home hero (reachability line + pulsing dot + the four tiles).

**SAY:**
> "Anyone can list agents. AgentLens tells you which ones are real, which are live, and which are worth hiring — by the job you need, verified in real time, on BNB Smart Chain. That's the marketplace the agent economy actually needs."

**End on the logo / URL.**

## Judging-criteria cheat sheet

Say at least one line that hits each. The scenes already cover them — this is just a pre-submit double-check.

| Criterion | Where you prove it | The line that lands it |
|---|---|---|
| **Functionality** (land → find → understand → hire, no dead ends) | Scenes 2→3→4 as one smooth flow | "one click to only reachable agents… one click to hire" |
| **Data Quality** (real-time, beyond basic counts) | Scenes 1 + 3 — 114/434 honest split, claims-vs-verified, "checked seconds ago" | "we show the difference between talk and proof" |
| **Agent Diversity** (all four categories) | Scenes 1 + 5 — four tiles, live counts per category | "all four jobs as first-class categories" |

⭐ The single most important thing: make **Scene 2 → 3 → 4** feel fast and smooth. That journey — find by category, see the proof, hire — *is* the submission.

---

## Do / Don't

**DO**
- Move at a calm, confident pace. A second of silence while a page loads is fine.
- Let the **animations breathe** — the gauge filling and the pulsing dots are the "wow."
- Use real numbers out loud ("about 114 of 434") — specifics feel true. Read them off your fresh page load.
- Show a **genuinely live** agent in Scenes 3–4 (that's why you scout one first).

**DON'T**
- Don't claim a feature you're not showing on screen.
- Don't call the health score a "reputation" or "rating" — it's a **live reachability check**. Keep that wording; it's the honest, defensible framing.
- Don't say the hire button sends an on-chain transaction — it hands over a **verified live connection**. The on-chain step is framed as "plugs in next." Stay honest.
- Don't rush Scene 2 → 3 → 4. That's the money shot.
- Don't record with notifications on.

## If a judge asks "why do Grid and Health have fewer agents?"

Answer with confidence — it's a strength, not a gap:
> "Because that's the real market on-chain today. There are only about a dozen genuine grid-trading and health-monitoring agents published on BNB right now — so that's what we show. We deliberately don't invent or pad agents to make a category look fuller. Showing the true supply, live-checked, is exactly the data honesty this marketplace is built on."
